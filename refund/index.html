Refund Policy

Effective date: October 1, 2026

Each purchase is considered separately based on its purchase date. You can request a refund from your account’s Purchase History or contact me by email.

Within 14 Days

Request a full refund within 14 days of purchase, no questions asked. Refunds requested within this window are typically processed within 1–2 business days.

Days 15–30

Refund requests made on days 15 through 30 after purchase are reviewed case by case and aren’t guaranteed.

After 30 Days

Refund requests aren’t available after 30 days from the purchase date.

How to Request a Refund

Sign in and use the refund button beside the relevant purchase in Purchase History, or email me with your account email and Paddle transaction ID.
