A little about why it exists

I built the tool I wanted at church.

Our service was already recorded. The sermon was already there. But getting just that portion ready to publish meant another round of scrubbing, trimming, exporting, and checking settings.

I wanted a calmer, more repeatable way to do that work—without sending our media to another service. So I started building SermonClip for my own workflow. I’m sharing it in case it helps with yours, too.

— Mike Church media director & SermonClip creator
